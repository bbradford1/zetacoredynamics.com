Flagship Product

AI Data Collect — Deep Dive

The operational analytics layer that sits on top of the AI cameras you already own — self-hosted, video-free, deployable in the places a VMS can't legally or technically reach.

What It Is

A self-hosted, enterprise-grade camera analytics platform that turns AI-capable IP cameras into a structured event stream — without ever recording, transcoding, or proxying video.

ADC ingests structured metadata — line crossings, occupancy counts, queue lengths, heatmaps, object detections — directly from the camera's onboard AI via MQTT and SFTP reports. No video on the wire, no GPU farm, no cloud upsell. Just the events that actually drive operational decisions, persisted in TimescaleDB on a box the customer owns.

The One-Line Pitch
"ADC is the operational analytics layer that sits on top of the AI cameras you already own — self-hosted, video-free, deployable in the places a VMS can't legally or technically reach."
📡

Bandwidth

Works on industrial LANs, cellular failover, satellite uplinks, and regulated networks where streaming video is forbidden or impractical.

💾

Storage

A full year of analytics fits where one week of 4K video would. 30/60/90-day retention is a deployment knob, not a hardware constraint.

⚖️

Compliance

No video means no privacy minefield. Banks, schools, cannabis retailers, government facilities, and corrections deploy without the legal review video systems trigger.

🔒

Air-Gap by Default

Designed to run with zero outbound internet. Internal CA, encrypted credentials at rest (Fernet), TLS-everywhere, signed-license offline activation in the roadmap.

What's Actually Built Today (Not Vapor)

One-Shot Install

install.sh with port pre-flight and auto-shift on collision, post-install shortcuts (adc-code, adc-ports), encrypted access codes, end-to-end first-run setup wizard.

🛡️

Hardened Auth

JWT + TOTP 2FA, invite-link onboarding, RBAC v2 with 14 named permissions and per-site data scoping enforced across 204 endpoints, full audit log.

📊

Customer Dashboard

5-tile health board, since-yesterday delta, sites rollup, multi-card camera-group occupancy with daily and weekly chart views.

🔔

Alerts Engine

Cooldown, snooze, multi-event-type, day-of-week schedule, multi-window, per-zone, friendly-name notifications via Slack, Email, Teams, and Webhooks. Per-alert JPEG snapshot capture.

🔍

Investigation Surface

Alert detail page with neighbor table, thumbnails, CSV export, deep-link from dashboard health tiles.

📷

Camera Driver Pack (CDP v2)

Driver-pack architecture handling Hanwha firmware variation across W5/W7/W8/W9/X+/fisheye/multisensor. Sandbox tester, capability oracle, signed validators. Designed to extend to Axis and Bosch.

Security Infrastructure

TLS-by-default MQTT (EMQX with internal CA), SFTPGo for camera reports, encrypted-at-rest credentials, 30/60/90 unified retention with automatic heatmap and snapshot purge, backup/restore with pre-restore validation.
